Title: ZIGRUSH

Short description:
Dodge obstacles, jump, speed up and chase a new skiing distance record.

Full description:
ZIGRUSH is a fast endless skiing arcade game built around simple one-finger controls.

Tap to change direction, hold to speed up, and swipe upward to jump over suitable obstacles. The farther you go, the faster and more challenging the run becomes.

Features:
• simple one-finger controls;
• gradually increasing speed and difficulty;
• trees, rocks and fences;
• jumping and boost mechanics;
• automatic best-distance saving;
• practice mode;
• one optional rewarded-video continue per run.
